# Business Loan Product

Product: FlexiBiz Business Loan (synthetic demo product)

- Purpose: working-capital financing for small and medium businesses.
- Loan amount: INR 2,00,000 to INR 25,00,000.
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months.
- Repayment: monthly instalments.
- Interest: risk-based; personalized pricing is confirmed only after application review.
- Disbursement: subject to approval, documentation, verification, and applicable checks.

Typical uses: inventory, expansion, equipment, working capital, short-term cash-flow needs.

Agent rule: distinguish general information, preliminary qualification, and final approval. Preliminary qualification is not approval or a guarantee of disbursement.
