# Loan FAQ

## What is the loan amount?
INR 2,00,000 to INR 25,00,000.

## What is the tenure?
12 to 60 months.

## How is interest decided?
Pricing is risk-based and personalized pricing is confirmed through application review. Do not invent a rate.

## How long must the business operate?
At least 2 years for preliminary qualification.

## What turnover is required?
Average monthly turnover of at least INR 3,00,000 for preliminary qualification.

## Is approval guaranteed?
No. Preliminary qualification is not final approval.

## What documents may be required?
Business proof, tax/business identification, address proof, recent bank statements, business financial information, and applicant identity proof.

## Can I get the money today?
Do not promise same-day disbursement; timing depends on approval, documentation, verification, and applicable checks.
