# Eligibility Policy

Synthetic demo rules.

Preliminary qualification requires:
- business operating for at least 2 years
- applicant age at least 21
- valid business registration or equivalent proof
- average monthly business turnover at least INR 3,00,000
- willingness to provide required documents
- requested amount between INR 2,00,000 and INR 25,00,000

Status values: `preliminarily_eligible`, `needs_more_information`, `not_preliminarily_eligible`, `human_requested`.

Hard rules:
- Never say the customer is finally approved.
- Never invent an interest rate, EMI, approval date, or disbursement date.
- Never collect OTPs, passwords, card PINs, or banking credentials.
- Unsupported questions must receive an explicit fallback and offer of human assistance.
