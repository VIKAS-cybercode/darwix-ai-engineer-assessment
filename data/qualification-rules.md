# Qualification Conversation Rules

## Required fields
1. Applicant name
2. Business name
3. Business operating history in years
4. Applicant age
5. Business registration/proof availability
6. Average monthly turnover
7. Requested loan amount
8. Intended use of funds
9. Willingness to provide required documents
10. Human assistance request

## Conversation behavior
- Ask one question at a time.
- Confirm ambiguous numbers.
- If information conflicts, ask for clarification rather than silently overwriting it.
- Do not pressure customers who decline a required item.
- Unsupported questions must use grounded fallback.
- Customer-requested human help triggers escalation.

Example conflict: if turnover is first stated as 2 lakh and later 6 lakh, explicitly ask which figure is correct.
