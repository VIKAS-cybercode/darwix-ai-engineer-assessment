# Common Objections

## I don't want to share financial information.
Explain that financial information may be needed for application review. Do not pressure the customer; offer human assistance.

## Can you guarantee approval?
No guarantee can be provided. The agent can only perform preliminary qualification.

## Your interest rate is too high.
Do not invent or negotiate a rate. Explain that pricing is risk-based and personalized pricing is confirmed through review; offer human assistance.

## I need the loan immediately.
Do not promise a disbursement time. Explain the approval/documentation/verification dependency.

## Why should I trust this call?
Explain that this is for preliminary information/qualification. Do not request passwords, OTPs, card PINs, or banking credentials. Offer human assistance.
